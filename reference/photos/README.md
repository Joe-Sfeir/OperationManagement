## Notes from the images seen so far (for M2 modelling)

- **Plan view.** The hub is at the centre and the cooking chamber is on the right. The round cooling chamber, with its fan, two ducts and the electric panel, is at the upper left. The yellow floor line runs from about 10 o'clock round the left and bottom to about 5 o'clock, and a red line encloses the oven side. The principal console sits outside the yellow line at the lower left, with the cooling console by the cooling chamber, the cooking console by the oven mouth and a second operator console at the bottom.
- **Plan view, stations.** The drawing seems to show six mould circles about 60° apart: the oven at 3 o'clock, cooling at about 11 o'clock, and four more at 1, 7, 9 and 5 o'clock. The bible's default is 4 arms on 5 positions. This is open; see docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md A8.
- **Cooking chamber (CAD).** Two blue sliding door panels hang on a top rail, with a yellow interior and a green floor. The roof platform has railings and a caged ladder.
- **Cooling chamber (CAD).** A blue cylindrical shell open on one side, yellow inside. It has a roof platform with railings, a stack on top and an angled side duct.
- **Hoist photo.** The real oven cabinet is cream panelling, unlike the blue doors in the CAD. In front of it stands a grey console with a red-screen HMI and a green light. An orange chain hoist on an overhead beam lifts a grey tank with a black top, and a cream platform has stairs. The dosing platform at the right holds two cream hoppers with a grey hose running down. The real floor is a brighter green than Floor epoxy #2C4638, which the bible darkened on purpose for the dark scene.
- **HMI page 0.** A schematic of the machine sits in the centre with a counter beside each arm. The panel also shows oven temperature (actual and set), the first-cycle minimum temperature, the manual or automatic cycle, the PLC link status and the operator name.
