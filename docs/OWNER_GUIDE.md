# Owner guide: how to use the Machine 3700 twin

For the person who runs the plant. No operations training is needed. Each step names the button or page to use.

1. **Watch a shift.** Open Machine and press Play. The machine runs one shift of Machine 3700. Choose a speed from 1x to 600x. Drag the bar at the bottom to jump to any moment; its colours show when the oven is heating.
2. **Move around the plant.** Drag to move around the plant, scroll to zoom, click a position for its details, and press Reset view to come back.
3. **Read the panels.** On the left, each arm shows what it is doing and how long is left, or how long it has waited. On the right are three figures: tanks out of the mould against the plan, how much of the shift the oven has been in use, and where arms are waiting now.
4. **Red ring, amber outline.** A red ring on the floor means that arm is waiting. An amber outline round the oven means arms are queuing for it: the oven is setting the pace.
5. **Change the set-up.** Press Set-up and choose the moulds on each arm. The shift is worked out again at once.
6. **Compare a redesign.** Open Redesign to see NTG's planned week beside another mix, with the tanks made and the stock at the end of the week for each.
7. **Plan next week.** Open Plan. NTG's numbers are filled in; change stock, demand or arm-days and read how many arm-cycles of each tank to schedule.
8. **See where a number comes from.** Press Sources in the top bar. A letter appears beside each figure: N means NTG told us, V means we saw it on a visit, C means it was calculated from those, S means it is an estimate and should be treated with care. With Sources off, hold the pointer over a figure to read the same information.

## Honest limits

The twin is calibrated, not validated. It reproduces the arm cycle and the shift output NTG reported, but nobody has timed a shift on the floor yet. One figure does not match: the twin has the last two arms of the day coming out after the end of the shift. The demould time is a placeholder until it is timed. Prices on the Sourcing page are researched estimates, not supplier quotes.

The full list is in three files beside this one: VALIDATION.md (what was checked and what was not), ASSUMPTIONS.md (every assumption with the alternative that was considered) and MODEL.md (how the simulator works).
